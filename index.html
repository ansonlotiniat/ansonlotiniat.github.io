01 / 工作方法Working method

介面之前，先找出資訊在哪裡斷開。 Before the interface, find where information breaks down.

我先理解使用者、角色與現場情境，再整理一次操作真正需要經過的步驟。 I first understand the people, roles, and real setting—then shape the steps an action actually needs.

一個工作流，由三次判斷開始。 A workflow begins with three decisions.

步驟 01 / 03 Step 01 / 03

01

觀察情境 Observe context

先看誰在使用，和事情發生在哪裡。 Start with who is using it—and where the work happens.

同一項資訊，在課室、賽場和團隊工作區裡，會有完全不同的優先次序。 The same information has different priorities in a classroom, at an event, or inside a team workspace.

PERSON PLACE TASK

02

整理流程 Shape the flow

把角色、任務和狀態放到正確順序。 Put roles, tasks, and states in the right order.

不是把所有功能塞進一個畫面，而是讓每個人只在需要時看見需要的內容。 Not every function belongs on one screen. Each person should see what they need, when they need it.

ROLE ACTION STATE NEXT

03

清楚交付 Deliver clearly

最後，讓下一步不需要猜。 Finally, make the next step impossible to miss.

清楚的狀態、克制的介面與合適的語氣，讓系統在真實環境裡自己說明。 Clear states, a restrained interface, and the right tone let the system explain itself in real use.

POEM / CONTRIBUTOR / 2026

Between Bells and Heartbeats

這首詩寫十六歲的日常：課堂、功課、訊息和說不清楚的感受。鐘聲安排學校生活，但人的感受不會跟着時間表走。

The poem is about ordinary life at sixteen: classes, homework, messages, and feelings that are difficult to articulate. School bells organise the day, but emotions do not follow a timetable.
