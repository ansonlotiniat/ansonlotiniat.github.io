03 / Working method

Principles with evidence

What I optimise for

Technical rigour,
with a human address.

01

Make evidence traceable

Record sources beside parameters. Prefer deterministic analysis. Make it possible for someone else to reproduce the result and disagree precisely.

02

Design the failure path

Safety is an interface. Add circuit breakers, audit trails, explicit limits, and a useful degraded state before treating a system as trustworthy.

03

Treat people as constraints

Accessibility, language, vulnerability, and unequal access are engineering requirements—not a paragraph added after the model is finished.
