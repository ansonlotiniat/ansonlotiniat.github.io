01

工作方法 Working method

OBSERVE / STRUCTURE / EXPLAIN

先整理流程，再畫介面。 Structure the flow before drawing the interface.

設計不是裝飾，
是把複雜的事說清楚。 Design is how I make
complex things clear.

我通常先問三件事：誰在使用、資訊在哪裡斷開、完成一次操作要走多少步。介面是最後一層，前面還有流程、權限、資料和現場情境。

I usually start with three questions: who is using this, where information breaks down, and how many steps it takes to finish a task. The interface is the final layer; before it come workflow, permissions, data, and the setting in which the tool is used.

演講學社的經驗也影響我做產品：先判斷對方需要知道甚麼，再決定順序和語氣。現在我是學社社長，仍然在練習怎樣讓不同的人一起把事情推進。

My work in the Speech Society shapes how I design products: decide what the audience needs, then set the order and tone. As its current president, I am still learning how to help different people move work forward together.

  1. 01

    觀察 Observe

    先看真實使用情境。 Start with the real setting.

  2. 02

    整理 Structure

    減少資訊和操作的阻力。 Reduce informational and operational friction.

  3. 03

    說明 Explain

    讓系統對人說人話。 Make the system speak plainly.

and words.

編輯讓我留意一段文字如何被安排；寫作讓我記得，清楚之外仍然要有人味。 Editing makes me notice how a piece of writing is arranged. Writing reminds me that clarity still needs a human voice.

Between Bells and Heartbeats

為學校英文詩集撰寫的作品。它寫十六歲的校園生活：鐘聲安排日程，感受卻有自己的時間。 Written for the school English poetry collection. It looks at being sixteen: school bells organise the day, while feelings keep their own time.
