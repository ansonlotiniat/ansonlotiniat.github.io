關於 About

我的興趣很分散，
但做事的方法差不多。 My interests are broad.
My way of working is consistent.

做軟件時，我會先理解實際流程：誰在使用、資訊從哪裡來、哪一個步驟最容易出錯。這也是我做校運會、辯論隊和校內比賽工具時的出發點。

When I build software, I start with the real workflow: who uses it, where information comes from, and which step is most likely to fail. That is how I approach tools for sports day, debate teams, and school competitions.

演講和帶領學社讓我習慣在有限時間內整理重點，也讓我更留意一件事應該怎樣向不同的人說明。

Public speaking and leading a school society have taught me to organise ideas under time pressure and explain the same issue to different people.

網絡、程式、歷史、數學和科學是我長期接觸的領域。它們沒有被我整理成一條單一路線，但會互相影響我理解問題的方式。

Networks, programming, history, mathematics, and science are fields I keep returning to. They do not form one neat track, but each changes how I understand a problem.
