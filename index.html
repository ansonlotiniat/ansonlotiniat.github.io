01 / 關於About

我從使用者真正要做的事開始。 I start with what people actually need to do.

畫介面之前，我會先看誰在使用、資訊在哪裡斷開，以及完成一次操作要走多少步。流程、權限、資料和現場情境處理好之後，介面才有意義。

Before drawing an interface, I look at who will use it, where information breaks down, and how many steps it takes to complete a task. The interface only becomes meaningful after workflow, permissions, data, and context are understood.

演講學社的工作也影響我做產品：先判斷對方需要知道甚麼，再決定順序和語氣。現在我是學社社長，仍然在學習怎樣讓不同的人一起把事情做好。

My work in the Speech Society also shapes how I build products: first decide what the audience needs, then choose the order and tone. As its current president, I am still learning how to help different people work well together.

  1. 01

    觀察情境 Observe context

    從真實使用方式開始。 Start from real behaviour.

  2. 02

    整理流程 Shape the flow

    減少資訊和操作阻力。 Reduce friction in information and action.

  3. 03

    清楚交付 Deliver clearly

    讓使用者知道下一步。 Make the next step obvious.

SCHOOL OPERATIONS / TWO YEARS

讓校運會資訊在需要時找得到。 Making sports-day information easy to find when it matters.

我連續兩年負責主要設計及開發，把報名、資訊發佈和現場查閱整理到同一個清楚入口。 For two years, I led design and development, bringing registration, updates, and on-site reference into one clear entry point.

使用情境 Context
校內大型活動，資訊隨時間持續更新。 A large school event with information changing over time.
我的角色 My role
主要介面設計與開發。 Lead interface design and development.
設計重點 Design focus
縮短查找路徑，讓現場使用不依賴說明。 Shorten the path to information and minimise on-site explanation.

TEAM + TOURNAMENT OPERATIONS

把隊務和賽務分開處理，再接回一起。 Separating team and tournament workflows, then reconnecting them.

我為學校辯論隊製作管理小程序，也為校內辯論比賽開發綜合管理系統。兩者服務不同的人，但需要共享清楚一致的資訊。 I built a management mini program for the school debate team and an integrated system for the internal tournament. They serve different people but depend on clear, consistent information.

使用情境 Context
日常隊務與一次性比賽並行。 Ongoing team operations alongside a time-bound tournament.
我的角色 My role
產品流程、介面與開發。 Product flow, interface, and development.
設計重點 Design focus
按角色整理任務、狀態與資訊。 Organise tasks, states, and information around each role.

POEM / CONTRIBUTOR / 2026

Between Bells and Heartbeats

這首詩寫十六歲的日常：課堂、功課、訊息和說不清楚的感受。鐘聲安排學校生活，但人的感受不會跟着時間表走。

The poem is about ordinary life at sixteen: classes, homework, messages, and feelings that are difficult to articulate. School bells organise the day, but emotions do not follow a timetable.
