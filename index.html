01 / PROFILE

我的興趣如何連在一起 How my interests connect

NOT A SINGLE LABEL

我不是一串獎項。
我在意的是，事情怎樣被做好。 I’m not a list of awards.
I care about how things get done.

A

為身邊的人做工具 Build for people around me

運動會、辯論隊、校內比賽——我最喜歡的軟件不是「看起來厲害」，而是有人真的會依靠它完成工作。

Sports day, debate teams, school competitions—the software I value most is not made to look impressive. It is made for people who genuinely rely on it.

B

把想法說清楚 Make ideas clear

即興演講、歷史寫作與社團帶領，訓練我在壓力下找到重點，讓不同背景的人願意聽、能夠理解。

Impromptu speaking, historical writing, and society leadership taught me to find the point under pressure—and make it understandable to different people.

C

刻意跨越邊界 Cross boundaries on purpose

網絡、程式、數學、化學、歷史看似分散；對我而言，它們都是理解複雜系統的不同入口。

Networks, code, mathematics, chemistry, and history may look separate. To me, they are different ways into the same question: how complex systems work.

04 / INTEREST MAP

我接下來會持續追問的問題 Questions I want to keep following

CURIOSITY IS A SYSTEM

我關心技術，
也關心技術進入世界之後會發生甚麼。 I care about technology—and what happens after it enters the world.

  1. 01

    團隊工具 Tools for teams 讓協作少一點混亂，多一點共同理解。 Less coordination noise; more shared understanding.

  2. 02

    網絡基礎設施 Network infrastructure 不只要能運作，還要能解釋、能維護。 Infrastructure that can be explained and maintained—not merely operated.

  3. 03

    科學中的計算工具 Computing for science 把複雜方法變得清楚，而不是再加一層炒作。 Make difficult methods clearer instead of adding another layer of hype.

  4. 04

    澳門文化的數碼表達 Digital expressions of Macao culture 讓世遺、非遺與地方記憶可以被探索和互動。 Make heritage, intangible culture, and local memory explorable.

  5. 05

    公共表達 Public communication 讓複雜議題可以被更多人討論，而不被過度簡化。 Open difficult questions to more people without flattening them.
