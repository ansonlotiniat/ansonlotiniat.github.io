FIELD 02 / VOICE

表達不是放大音量。
是重新安排注意力。 Voice is not more volume.
It is ordered attention.

01 / AUDIENCE

先判斷誰在聽。First, know who is listening.

聽眾需要甚麼，決定了資訊從哪裡開始。What the audience needs determines where the information begins.

02 / ORDER

把重點放在正確順序。Put the emphasis in the right order.

演講訓練和比賽準備，讓我更在意一件事應該何時被聽見。Speech training and competition preparation made me attentive to when an idea should be heard.

03 / TONE

清楚，但不要抹走人的語氣。Be clear without erasing the person.

社團協作、演講與編輯，都需要在準確和原有聲音之間取得平衡。Society work, speaking, and editing all balance precision with the original voice.
